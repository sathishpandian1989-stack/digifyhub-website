Ask a Question

Common questions

What services does Digifyhub provide?

We offer SEO and local SEO, Google Search Ads, Meta Ads for Facebook and Instagram, social media management and content planning, website and landing page development, and lead generation strategy with enquiry tracking. See our services page for details.

How do we get started?

Fill in the short enquiry form, call us or send a WhatsApp message. We'll arrange a free consultation to understand your business and goals, then send a clear proposal with recommended next steps, deliverables and costs.

How soon can results appear?

It depends on the channel. Paid advertising such as Google or Meta Ads can start bringing traffic soon after launch, while SEO usually builds over several months. Outcomes vary by industry, competition, budget and execution, so we'll set realistic expectations for your situation — and we never guarantee specific results.

What information is needed for a proposal?

Your business name, what you offer, your target customers, your goals and an idea of your budget. Links to your website and social profiles, and any past marketing data, help us make more specific recommendations.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Yes — small businesses, startups and aspiring entrepreneurs are exactly who we're built to help. We tailor the scope to your stage and budget. If you're just starting out, our Entrepreneur Hub is a good place to begin.

How are campaign results reported?

You'll receive regular, plain-language reports covering the work completed, key results such as enquiries, traffic and spend, and our recommendations for the next period. We agree on the goals and metrics that matter at the start.

How much does digital marketing cost?

Pricing depends on the services, scope and advertising budget involved. After the free consultation we'll send a proposal with clear costs, so you know exactly what you're paying for before anything starts.

How do we communicate during a project?

By whichever works best for you — phone, email or WhatsApp — plus scheduled check-ins and reports. You'll always know what we're working on and what's coming next.

Digital marketing outcomes vary by industry, competition, budget and execution. We focus on clear goals, consistent work and honest reporting rather than guaranteed results.

Ready to turn your idea into growth?

Tell us where your business is today and where you want it to go. We'll suggest a clear, practical next step.
