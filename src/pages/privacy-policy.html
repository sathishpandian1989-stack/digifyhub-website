Privacy Policy

How Digifyhub collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Who we are

Digifyhub ("we", "us") is a digital marketing and business growth agency. You can contact us about this policy at {{email}} or {{phone}}.

What we collect

When you submit our enquiry form, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and business name
  • The service you're interested in and any message you choose to write
  • Your consent confirmation, the page you submitted the form from, and the date and time of submission

We also collect basic technical information needed to keep the form secure and prevent spam and duplicate submissions.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation
  • To prepare a proposal tailored to your business
  • To follow up on your enquiry by phone, email or WhatsApp
  • To keep our website secure and prevent misuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your permission.

How it is stored

Enquiries are stored securely in a private Google Sheet that only authorised Digifyhub team members can access, and a notification is sent to our business email. These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any working relationship, and then delete them when they are no longer required. Technical logs are kept for a limited period and contain as little personal data as possible.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the information you've shared with us. Just email {{email}}.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, we'll update this policy to explain what is collected.

Third-party links

Our website links to services such as WhatsApp, Facebook and LinkedIn. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
